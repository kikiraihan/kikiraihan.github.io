## Brief

A modern, minimalist logo that also tells the history of a company founded through a student's hard work.

## Solution

The mark is built on the **golden ratio** (1.618), so every angle and plane stays balanced — that is the "modern and minimalist". Its shape is a bag being dragged; behind it, a tail of wind forms a Fibonacci curve. The bag is the student; the curve is every struggle, turned into a beautiful stroke.
