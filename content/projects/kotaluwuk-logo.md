## Brief

A modern, minimalist logo for a tourism website, using Luwuk's icon — the maleo bird.

## Solution

A maleo bird in front of a sun and clouds. The clouds sit *below* the bird, so it appears to fly high — Luwuk is not only beautiful, but also wise and advanced.
