## Brief

Present GenBI as a community with a clear vision and a directed target, with a design that is easy to remember — a first step toward the 2025 target, starting from internal management and GenBI Gorontalo's social-media presence.

## Solution

The 2025 vision and values were formulated as:

> "Becoming the best new generation of Indonesia in Sulawesi, holding the values of kinship, mutual protection, determination, professionalism, and activeness (KESATRIA)."

That vision became a logo, repeated across GenBI Gorontalo posts as a reminder.

![Community vision logo](/images/work/genbi-branding/logo-visi-kesatria-2025.png)

Each division also received its own symbol:

1. **Core Management** — a dot with a line piercing the circle: a connector that breaks through every obstacle toward the goal.
2. **Entrepreneurship** — dots and lines shaped like people helping each other.
3. **Religion and Education** — a shape representing guidance and knowledge.
4. **Public Health** — a dot inside a circle: care and protection.
5. **Environment** — a small leaf with root-like lines: cleanliness and freshness.
6. **Organization and Membership** — a dot with one line piercing the circle, embracing members and removing partitions.
7. **Cooperation and Partnership** — fast-flowing water: the spirit to explore, the spearhead of external relations.

![Division symbols](/images/work/genbi-branding/simbol-bidang-departemen.png)

All of it was applied across activity templates and infographics.
