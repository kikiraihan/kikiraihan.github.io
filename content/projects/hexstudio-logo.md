## Brief

A studio logo that is modern yet friendly, and signals design expertise.

## Solution

Negative space forms the word **HEX**. The palette comes from the three primaries of digital design — red, green and blue — signalling an understanding of design fundamentals. The tones are calm rather than bright, to keep the impression friendly.
