## Brief

A modern, minimalist logo for a tourism website, using Luwuk's icon — the maleo bird.

## Solution

A maleo bird in front of a sun and clouds. The clouds sit *below* the bird, so it appears to fly high — Luwuk is not only beautiful, but also wise and advanced.

![Kotaluwuk.com logo](/images/work/kotaluwuk-logo/logo-kotaluwuk.png)

## Reading the mark

The emblem is meant to be read top to bottom, like a postcard: sky, subject, horizon. Every element is either a solid white shape or a thin white line, so the whole scene holds together in one color.

![Reading the Kotaluwuk.com mark from top to bottom](/images/work/kotaluwuk-logo/kotaluwuk-layers.svg)
