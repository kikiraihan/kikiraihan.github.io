## Brief

A studio logo that is modern yet friendly, and signals design expertise.

## Solution

Negative space forms the word **HEX**. The palette comes from the three primaries of digital design — red, green and blue — signalling an understanding of design fundamentals. The tones are calm rather than bright, to keep the impression friendly.

![HEXSTUDIO logo](/images/work/hexstudio-logo/logo-hexstudio.png)

## How the letters appear

Nothing in the mark is drawn as a letter. Each one is made of simple blocks, and the letter only exists in the white space they leave behind — the kind of detail a design client notices, and a quiet proof that the studio thinks about what *isn't* there.

![How the HEXSTUDIO mark spells H, E and X with negative space](/images/work/hexstudio-logo/hexstudio-negative-space.svg)

  - { title: Start from the name, text: '"HEX" is short and geometric — a good candidate for letters built from blocks rather than drawn.' }
  - { title: "Blocks, not strokes", text: "Rectangles and triangles only: crisp at any size and easy to reproduce in any medium." }
  - { title: Carve the letters out, text: "Cut gaps, slots and channels until H, E and X appear in the negative space." }
  - { title: One primary per letter, text: "Red, green and blue — the RGB of every screen — each softened so the mark feels approachable." }

## Variants

The wordmark is set in widely tracked capitals with **DIGITAL ART STUDIO** as a smaller descriptor underneath. Because the letters live in the negative space, the mark survives being reduced to a single color — it still reads as HEX in light gray on charcoal.
