## Brief

A modern, minimalist logo that also tells the history of a company founded through a student's hard work.

## Solution

The mark is built on the **golden ratio** (1.618), so every angle and plane stays balanced — that is the "modern and minimalist". Its shape is a bag being dragged; behind it, a tail of wind forms a Fibonacci curve. The bag is the student; the curve is every struggle, turned into a beautiful stroke.

![Katili Corp logo explanation](/images/work/katili-corp-logo/logo-katili-corp.png)

## The story in two shapes

The whole mark is one idea split into two parts: what the student carries, and the road behind them.

## Construction

"Minimalist" is easy to say and hard to defend. Here it is defended by one rule: every curve and proportion comes from the Fibonacci sequence, the whole-number approximation of 1 : 1.618.

![The Fibonacci squares and golden spiral behind the Katili Corp mark](/images/work/katili-corp-logo/katili-fibonacci.svg)
